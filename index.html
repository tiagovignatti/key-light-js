Add Key Light to your Home Screen

On iPhone, Safari can't make a page fullscreen. Opened from the Home Screen, Key Light fills the screen with no browser bars.

  1. Tap Share . If you don't see it, tap next to the address first.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen . If it isn't listed, scroll down or tap View More.
  3. Leave Open as Web App on, then tap Add.
  4. Open Key Light from your Home Screen.
